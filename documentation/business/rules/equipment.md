# Equipment Rules

## Confirmed business rule

| ID | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| BR-EQ-001 | Any supported equipment type can be associated with either a building or a housing unit. Equipment type must not restrict the kind of property to which it can be associated. | Confirmed by stakeholder direction |

This rule defines the required business capability. It does not prescribe how the application stores the association or how equipment-specific behavior is implemented.

## Type-specific behavior

Equipment types may have different attributes, lifecycle rules, maintenance or inspection histories, and alerts. The shared ability to associate equipment with a building or housing unit does not imply that every type must support identical behavior.

See [Property Equipment and Assets](../concepts/property-equipment.md) for the types currently represented in the application.
