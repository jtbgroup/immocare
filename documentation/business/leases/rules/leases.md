# Business Rules — Leases

These rules describe lease constraints established by confirmed business policy. The lease application behavior and persisted fields are documented in the [technical lease references](../../../technical/leases/README.md).

| ID | Rule | Status |
|---|---|---|
| BR-LEASE-001 | A regulated reference rent is not directly associated with a lease in the domain model; external regulation may govern lease terms without creating that relationship. | Confirmed |

BR-LEASE-001 was confirmed by the user on 2026-11-07. Which external lease-regulation schemes and jurisdictions the application should support remains an open question; see [Open Questions](../../../../backlog/open_questions.md#housing-units-and-leases).
