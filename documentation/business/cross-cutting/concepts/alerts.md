# Concept — Alerts

Alerting is cross-cutting: an alert may arise from different business domains, including equipment, lease events, or energy-performance (PEB) information. This shared classification does not imply that every alert type is currently implemented or define its trigger, timing, recipient, or lifecycle.

The current estate dashboard reports pending boiler, fire-extinguisher, lease-end, and indexation alert counts. Its implementation behavior is documented in [Estate Management](../../../technical/estates-and-access/estate-management.md). Domain-specific alert criteria belong with the relevant domain; shared alert presentation and lifecycle belong in cross-cutting documentation.
