# Business Glossary

Concise definitions of terms used in this reference. Structurally important concepts are described in more detail in the [concept pages](./concepts/).

| Term                       | Concise definition                                                                                                                        | Details                                                           |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Accounting month           | The month to which a transaction is assigned for tracking and financial reporting; it may differ from its bank transaction date.          | [Financial transaction](./concepts/financial-transaction.md)      |
| Boiler                     | A heating appliance tracked by its installation, service dates, and service history.                                                      | [Property equipment and assets](./concepts/property-equipment.md) |
| Building                   | A property in an estate that may contain housing units.                                                                                   | [Estate and rental](./concepts/estate-and-rental.md)              |
| Category / subcategory     | Hierarchical classification levels used to classify transactions.                                                                         | [Financial transaction](./concepts/financial-transaction.md)      |
| Estate                     | A management scope to which the application assigns property and related data.                                                            | [Estate and access](./concepts/estate-and-access.md)               |
| Estate membership          | The association that grants a user account a role within a specific estate.                                                                | [Estate and access](./concepts/estate-and-access.md)               |
| Financial transaction      | An inflow or outflow recorded in an estate, with a date, amount, and direction.                                                           | [Financial transaction](./concepts/financial-transaction.md)      |
| Fire extinguisher          | Fire-safety equipment that can be associated with a building or a housing unit and has a revision history.                                | [Property equipment and assets](./concepts/property-equipment.md) |
| Housing unit               | A dwelling or unit within a building that may be associated with a lease.                                                                 | [Estate and rental](./concepts/estate-and-rental.md)              |
| Lease                      | A rental relationship associated with a housing unit and one or more people.                                                              | [Estate and rental](./concepts/estate-and-rental.md)              |
| Manager / viewer           | Estate-scoped application access roles; they do not, by themselves, define business roles.                                                  | [Roles](./roles.md)                                               |
| Meter                      | A utility meter associated with a building or housing unit and tracked over a period of use.                                              | [Property equipment and assets](./concepts/property-equipment.md) |
| Person                     | An individual recorded within the management scope who may own property or be associated with a lease.                                    | [Estate and rental](./concepts/estate-and-rental.md)              |
| Platform administrator     | A user account with platform-wide administrative access, separate from estate-specific membership roles.                                  | [User account](./concepts/user-account.md)                        |
| Property equipment / asset | A tracked physical item associated with a building or housing unit, such as a boiler, fire extinguisher, or meter.                        | [Property equipment and assets](./concepts/property-equipment.md) |
| Room                       | A space or room represented as part of a housing unit.                                                                                    | [Estate and rental](./concepts/estate-and-rental.md)              |
| Service / revision         | A recorded maintenance or inspection event for tracked equipment.                                                                         | [Property equipment and assets](./concepts/property-equipment.md) |
| Transaction-to-asset link  | A relationship that associates a financial transaction with tracked equipment, optionally assigning part of the transaction amount to it. | [Financial transaction](./concepts/financial-transaction.md)      |
| User account               | An application identity used to sign in; it is distinct from a person record.                                                              | [User account](./concepts/user-account.md)                        |
